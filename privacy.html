Privacy Policy – HAJR Quran Publisher

Last updated: November 10, 2026

HAJR Quran Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of HAJR Quran to publish HAJR Quran's own videos to its own TikTok and other social media accounts.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or process personal data from any other users. The only data handled is the OAuth access token issued for the owner's own account, used solely to publish content to that account.

How data is used and stored

The token is stored securely on the owner's server and is never sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics. No data is shared with third parties other than the platform the content is published to.

Deletion

The owner may revoke access at any time from the platform's app settings; the stored token is then deleted.
